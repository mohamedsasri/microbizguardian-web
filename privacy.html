Privacy notice

Last updated: November 2026

What we collect

If you join the waitlist or contact us, we collect your name, email address and type of business. We do not collect payment details or business records through this website.

Why we collect it

To tell you when MicroBiz Guardian early access opens, to ask for feedback, and to reply to your messages. We only collect data you choose to give us, and only with your consent.

How it is stored and shared

Your details are held by the project team in our form-handling service and are not sold or shared with third parties for marketing. Any analytics we use only count visits and sign-ups.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete your information, or withdraw consent at any time, by emailing hello@microbizguardian.lk. We handle personal data in line with Sri Lanka’s Personal Data Protection Act, No. 9 of 2022.

About this project

MicroBiz Guardian is a student project at the University of Westminster and is still in development.
