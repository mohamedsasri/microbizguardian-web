Small shops record everything, but still miss what it means.

Sales sit in one app, stock in a notebook and expenses in a spreadsheet. Owners end up piecing the story together themselves.

  • Problems are noticed lateA slow decline in sales or a rising cost is easy to miss day to day.
  • Stock is guessworkItems run out, or money sits in products that do not sell.
  • Reports are hard to readMost tools show numbers, not what to do about them.
  • Tools speak one languageFew systems work comfortably in Sinhala or Tamil.

One system that connects the dots.

MicroBiz Guardian links checkout, stock, suppliers and expenses to an intelligence layer that explains what is changing and why.

  1. Record
    Sell, restock and log expenses as usual.
  2. Understand
    See one health score and the evidence behind every warning.
  3. Decide
    Ask the Copilot, then choose your next step. You stay in control.

Everything a shop needs, plus the insight most tools skip.

Core POS tools and an AI layer, designed together from the start. Screens below are from our prototype design.

Business Health Score

One number built from sales, profit, expenses, stock and cash flow, so you know at a glance whether things are improving.

AI Business Copilot

Ask questions in everyday language and get answers linked to your own records. If data is missing, it tells you.

Early warnings

Stock shortages, falling sales and profit changes flagged with the reasons.

Anomaly detection

Spots unusual discounts, refunds, expenses and stock gaps for you to review. It never accuses anyone.

Smart POS

Fast billing, returns and transaction history with stock updated automatically.

Inventory and suppliers

Reorder suggestions, plus supplier price and delivery insights.

Profit and cash flow

Expense tracking, profit reports and cash-flow forecasts.

Works in your language

Switch the interface and assistant between English, Sinhala and Tamil.

Honest about gaps
